Net Shipping
Shipping is signed,
and it varies widely.
Net shipping is what the carrier bills you minus what the customer pays you.
We charge a flat $9.99 under $100, free over $100.
This applies to sites with a Free Shipping threshold.
Paid shipping and Always-Free shipping models have their own compromises.
